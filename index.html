Broader Potential

Other Conditions Mitapivat May Treat

PKR activation extends beyond thalassemia — broadening the potential patient population and PBS economic case.

Pyruvate Kinase (PK) Deficiency
Rare haemolytic anaemia · ~3–9 per million globally
✓ FDA & EMA Approved
Mitapivat's original indication. ACTIVATE and ACTIVATE-T Phase 3 trials met all endpoints. Pediatric ACTIVATE-Kids met primary endpoint Feb 2025.
🇦🇺 Est. ~70–230 Australian patients
Sickle Cell Disease (SCD)
Affects red cell sickling and haemolysis
⏳ Phase 2/3 — RISE UP Trial
Phase 2 showed Hb increases and reduced pain crisis frequency. If approved, significantly expands PBS patient population.
🇦🇺 Est. ~4,000–8,000 Australian patients
Alpha-Thalassemia (HbH disease)
Most prevalent in Southeast Asian heritage Australians
✓ Included in EU/FDA Approval 2025–26
Both alpha and beta thalassemia (TD and NTD) are included in the May 2026 EU and Dec 2025 FDA approvals.
🇦🇺 Significant overlap with TD thalassemia estimate
Other Hereditary Haemolytic Anaemias
Broader PKR activation benefit shown in research
🔬 Early Research Stage
PKR activation can benefit a range of hereditary haemolytic conditions. Second-generation activators (tebapivat) under development.
🇦🇺 Potential additional thousands of patients
